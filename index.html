STORY LANGUAGEKAHANI KI ZABAN
01 / THE BEGINNING01 / SHURUAAT✦

Kohat Zalmi is more than just a cricket team; it is a story of childhood friendship, passion and brotherhood.

Our journey began many years ago when we were just a group of childhood friends who shared one common passion: cricket. Growing up in Pakistan, where tape-ball cricket is a huge part of our culture, we spent countless hours playing together, creating memories and enjoying every moment on the field.

As time passed, our passion for cricket grew stronger. What started as casual tape-ball matches slowly turned into a bigger dream. Together, we decided to take the next step and move from tape-ball cricket to hard-ball cricket.

That decision marked the beginning of a new chapter in our journey.

Today, we stand together as Kohat Zalmi, carrying the same friendship, unity and passion that brought us together in childhood. We may have changed the ball, the format and the level of competition, but one thing has never changed: our bond as friends.

Kohat Zalmi sirf aik cricket team nahi; yeh bachpan ki dosti, junoon aur bhaicharay ki kahani hai.

Hamara safar kai saal pehle shuru hua, jab hum bachpan ke doston ka aik group thay aur hum sab ka aik hi mushtarka shauq tha: cricket. Pakistan mein, jahan tape-ball cricket hamari culture ka aik bara hissa hai, hum ghanton aik saath khelte, yaadein banate aur ground par har lamha enjoy karte thay.

Waqt guzarnay ke saath cricket ke liye hamara junoon aur mazboot hota gaya. Jo shuru mein casual tape-ball matches thay, woh dheere dheere aik baray khwab mein badal gaye. Hum sab ne mil kar agla qadam uthane aur tape-ball cricket se hard-ball cricket ki taraf janay ka faisla kiya.

Yeh faisla hamare safar ke aik naye baab ki shuruaat tha.

Aaj hum Kohat Zalmi ke naam se aik saath kharay hain aur wohi dosti, ittehad aur cricket ka junoon apne saath liye hue hain jis ne humein bachpan mein jora tha. Ball, format aur muqablay ka level badal gaya, lekin doston ke taur par hamara rishta kabhi nahi badla.
